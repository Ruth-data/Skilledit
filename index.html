What Exactly Is Ghostwriting?

Ghostwriting is the practice of creating written content for individuals, professionals or businesses, usually without public authorship credit. The client publishes the work under their own name, while the ghostwriter does the writing behind the scenes. It is a skill, not a shortcut, and it is built through practice.

📝

Blog articles

Helpful posts that share a person’s or brand’s ideas.

📚

Books and ebooks

Longer projects that turn expertise or stories into a finished book.

🎤

Speeches

Words written to be spoken aloud, in someone else’s voice.

📱

Social media content

Short, clear posts that match a brand’s tone.

📬

Newsletters

Regular emails that keep readers informed and engaged.

💼

Business content

Web pages, reports and guides written for companies.

Not every ghostwriter earns the same, and income is never guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ghostwriting?

Ghostwriting is writing content, such as articles, books, speeches or social posts, that another person or business publishes under their own name.

Can beginners learn ghostwriting?

Yes. Writing is a skill that improves with practice and feedback. How quickly you progress, and whether you find paid work, varies from person to person.

Do I need previous writing experience?

Not necessarily. Comfort with written English and a willingness to practise are a good start. Check the course provider for any specific requirements.

Can I learn ghostwriting using my smartphone?

You can practise writing and read learning material on a phone. Longer projects are often easier on a laptop with a keyboard. Whether this course works well on mobile needs to be confirmed with the course provider.

Is ghostwriting the same as copywriting?

No, but they overlap. Ghostwriting means writing in someone else’s voice, usually without credit, across many formats. Copywriting focuses on persuasive writing for marketing and sales.
